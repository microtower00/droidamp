Initial alpha pre-release (v0.1.0-alpha.1):
- Media3 foreground playback service with custom inline PCM DSP (10-band Winamp EQ, ReplayGain, gapless, crossfade, 60-120fps VFD visualizer).
- Stereo-tower 1:1 pan UI with bottom thumb-zone Queue/Library tabs, iPod-style Library root, and Save Queue as .m3u8/.pls dialog.
- Full backend Winamp format codecs (.eqf, winamp.q1/q2, winamp.ini, .m3u/.m3u8/.pls) and AUTO EQ matching.
